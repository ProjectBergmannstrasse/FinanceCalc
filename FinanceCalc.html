Bank & Trading
By default your trading money and your bank money are one pot, so wins and losses move the same number you'd spend from. Split them and “Money I have right now” becomes actual liquid cash, with trading capital tracked separately.

Tax Set-Aside
Money parked for tax leaves your spendable balance and sits in its own pot. It's left out of net worth on purpose — it was never really yours. Use Set aside for tax and Pay tax bill in the entry form.

Danger Zone
Wipes every entry in the portfolio you're currently in. Your other portfolios are untouched. A full backup downloads automatically first.
Recent actions
Changes from this session, newest first. Undoing one also undoes anything after it.
Recently deleted
Deleted entries wait here for 30 days — photos and all — before they go for good. Restore anything that shouldn't have gone.
Savings goals
Measured against your real savings pot. The forecast uses what you've actually been saving, not what you'd like to.
Repeats
Rent, salary, subscriptions. Nothing is ever added silently — when something's due you get asked on the dashboard first.

Monthly budgets
Set a monthly cap per category. Leave one blank for no cap. Caps always measure the current calendar month, whatever months you have ticked elsewhere.
Import trades from a file
Paste an MT4/MT5 statement or any CSV. It works out the columns, shows you what it found, and skips anything already in your ledger.
Wording
Call things whatever you actually call them. Leave a box empty to use the default.
Layout
Put each card where you want it, or hide it. Use ▲▼ to reorder. Takes effect straight away.
